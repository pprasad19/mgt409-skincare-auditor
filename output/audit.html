SKA-001unverified ruleConflict
Retinol 0.5% in Squalane + Glycolic Acid 7% Toning Solution · Retinol 1% in Squalane + Glycolic Acid 7% Toning Solution

Using glycolic acid with either retinol can add irritation and trigger pigmentation.

Why
Both increase cell turnover and both disrupt the barrier. Used in the same session the irritation is additive, and the resulting inflammation can drive the pigmentation many users bought the acid to treat.

Do this: Use on alternate nights rather than together.
SKA-003unverified ruleConflict
Retinol 0.5% in Squalane + Acne Foaming Wash 4% · Retinol 1% in Squalane + Acne Foaming Wash 4%

Acne Foaming Wash 4% breaks down either retinol when used together, wasting the retinol.

Why
Benzoyl peroxide is an oxidiser and most retinoids oxidise readily. Applied together, a measurable fraction of the retinoid is degraded before it acts, so the more expensive product is the one being wasted.

Do this: Separate them — benzoyl peroxide in the morning, retinoid at night.
SKA-006unverified ruleDuplicate
Retinol 0.5% in Squalane + Retinol 1% in Squalane

The two retinols don’t add benefit together, but they do add irritation.

Why
Two retinoids do not compound the benefit but do compound the irritation. This frequently happens by accident, because retinyl palmitate appears in moisturisers that are not marketed as retinoid products.

Do this: Keep the stronger one. Check moisturiser labels for a second retinoid you did not know about.

SKA-005unverified ruleCaution
Retinol 0.5% in Squalane + C E Ferulic · Retinol 1% in Squalane + C E Ferulic

C E Ferulic and either retinol work best at different times; layering them can irritate skin.

Why
L-ascorbic acid needs a pH near 3 and retinoids work closer to 5.5. Layered directly, neither sits in its effective range, and the combined acid load irritates.

Do this: Vitamin C in the morning, retinoid at night. They are not incompatible, just badly matched in one session.
SKA-010unverified ruleCaution
Glycolic Acid 7% Toning Solution + 2% BHA Liquid Exfoliant

Using glycolic acid and the BHA exfoliant together can overwhelm your skin barrier.

Why
Combined exfoliation easily exceeds what the barrier can repair between uses. Tolerable for some skin, but it is the most common cause of the over-exfoliation state users mistake for sensitivity.

Do this: Use on different days unless your skin has clearly tolerated both together.
